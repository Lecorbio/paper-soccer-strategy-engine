Interpretation note

The ratings are tournament-specific

The games follow the CodinGame Paper Soccer rules and timing contract, but CodinGame does not publish its complete matchmaking and rating configuration. These TrueSkill values are therefore comparable within this local tournament—not interchangeable with live CodinGame league scores.

Historical CodinGame results remain in each bot’s documentation and are never mixed into the local-score column.
