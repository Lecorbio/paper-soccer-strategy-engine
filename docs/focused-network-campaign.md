# Focused network research

The successor begins with a common deployment and training pipeline, compares
degree-aware 8/8 and 12/16 networks under matched conditions, then retains one
family for cumulative development. A failed promotion test retains its useful
checkpoints, data and implementation findings.

The initial recipe uses two paired seeds, root-balanced teacher Huber plus
0.25 ranking loss, float training before four-bit QAT, and training-only scale
calibration. Incomplete successor enumeration cannot create exhaustive ranking
targets. Mover signs and exact proof/terminal labels remain explicit. Root
checkpoints preserve parameters, optimizer moments, step count and data order.

The exporter retains the verified alpha-beta implementation and compacts its
tokens using reversible aliases. An independent compiler token stream checks
the transformation in normal, probe and conditional compiler configurations.
Packed four-bit and Huffman payloads share a precisely defined Base85 transport;
the smaller complete source is selected. Exception diagnostics are retained in
the export report rather than repeated in the submitted source.

Exports are standalone translation units. Include-based native adapters must
append the report's alias cleanup before their own code. Codec correctness,
tensor equality and fixed-work equivalence are separate from isolated response
timing and playing-strength qualification.

Preparation lasts at most four days, followed by at most three days for a
256-root matched study. Its four seed checkpoints and exact incumbent control
share opponents, roots, colors and response envelopes. The study uses whole-root
bootstrap intervals and favors 8/8 unless 12/16 has a positive lower 95% bound.
The result is conditional on the frozen seeds and panel.

The fourteen-day clock starts once after the predecessor audit. Promotion keeps
the existing pilot, development, private, formal and fresh confirmation gates.
The incumbent is retained until qualification; offline fixtures and compiler
checks never establish playing strength. Campaign artifacts, game transcripts,
data, checkpoints and host-specific control state remain local.

These panels differ, so the table does not establish a causal ranking of the
interventions. The 8/8 family was chosen for feasibility after the wider family
failed a cold-response safety check; no width superiority claim was established.

The final round froze a half-uniform, half-hard loss mixture inside each TRAIN
root. Hard groups required exhaustive enumeration, initial-float teacher regret
at least 0.05 and best teacher value greater than −0.95. Nonexhaustive groups
could not enter the hard subset. All 512 TRAIN roots still received an update
per epoch; the 128 validation roots were neither weighted nor used to calibrate
scales. The completed regular arm was an immutable training reference, ineligible
for promotion. Only the hard arm was trained from their common useful float
checkpoint and optimizer moments. All 19 epoch checkpoints and 9,728 root
checkpoints were retained; the float cap was budget limited.

The hard arm improved the frozen native selection tuple: regret
0.0459102910 versus 0.0470809169 and teacher Huber 0.0168268745 versus
0.0172199947. Its five mechanical proofs and isolated 550/140 ms response checks
passed. Nevertheless, its complete paired playing panel was weaker than the
incumbent. All 256 games and 9,661 decisions were replayed for protocol
verification. This is a strength stop, not an operational source ban.

The study used a frozen offline roster, including a Jacek-style proxy. It used
neither actual live Jacek/Marchete sources nor qualification games as training
data. It therefore establishes no improvement against those live opponents.
The useful conclusion is that better teacher metrics alone did not justify
deployment. Further work would need a separately approved experiment and an
independent playing test; no fifth round or replacement checkpoint is admitted
by this campaign.

All spent games, proposals, recipes and diagnostic exposures have retained
exclusion ancestry. Storage recovery preserved logical bytes and hashes. A
small terminal-only SQLite overlay records the passive replay visible during
the deployment audit alongside the unchanged full prior; it grants no new
training or bank admission. Private receipts, transcripts and checkpoint data
remain local.
