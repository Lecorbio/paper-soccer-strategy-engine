# Live-led 8/8 research extension

The extension uses live CodinGame exploration to improve the existing bias-free
6,301 → 8 → 8 → 1 value network. It preserves the original campaign deadline,
21 October 2026 at 23:28 Warsaw time, with finalist selection 48 hours earlier.
The twelve experimental live-version slots include one retimed incumbent
control; two slots remain reserved until the final four days.

The previous offline strength failures remain evidence about their frozen
roster. Exploration requires source-bound correctness and operational safety,
without an offline score veto. Final deployment requires an independent live
comparison, a private panel and one consecutive three-window formal block.
The retimed incumbent may qualify if it remains the strongest tested source.

Every upload has a declared question and immutable source. Exploration uses
B–C–C–B, where B is the original incumbent. One B–C extension is available for
insufficient coverage. Six primary opponents are fixed from the current ladder:
Jacek, Marchete and the strongest four other opponents. Comparisons match
opponent submission versions and colors, weight opponents and colors equally,
and bootstrap paired complete calibration windows rather than independent
individual games. All unmatched games remain descriptive evidence.

A confidence interval requires all 10,000 whole-window resamples to contain the
required opponent and color cells. If any resample is unidentified, report no
interval rather than condition on the supported subset. Exploration results
cannot replace the independent qualification blocks. Own operational failures
in later games also block promotion, even when the initial calibration is clean.

Only new games explicitly designated as exploration may supply training
positions. Teacher evaluations of alternative actions provide targets; a losing
outcome is not assigned to every preceding position. Retained TRAIN roots and
live TRAIN roots each receive half the loss mass. Related positions and
reflections stay together. Fresh validation has 128 roots, with at least 100
comparable early roots. The cumulative training limit is 2,048 roots.

The initial teacher budget is 256k nodes. A separately declared TRAIN-only
intervention may use up to 1M nodes. Continuation caps are 80 float and 32 QAT
epochs, with existing minimums and plateau stopping. Every epoch checkpoint,
selected parent and diagnostic is retained. Atomic current-state checkpoints
and an append-only update journal permit exact resume without retaining a new
full historical checkpoint after every root. All old checkpoints stay intact.

Four-bit and six-bit quantization are compared from a common float checkpoint.
Seven-bit quantization requires source feasibility and better frozen native
validation. The source target is 99,000 ASCII characters, below the 100,000
hard limit. Timing tuples are 550/140, 750/160, 900/170 and 990/180 milliseconds.
Final sources require codec/tensor checks, native float parity, compiler token
proofs, fixed-work equivalence for runtime changes and isolated production
cold/warm/long-turn response certification.

The finalist is frozen before two fresh B–C–C–B blocks, with one coverage-only
B–C extension if needed. Each primary opponent needs at least eight clean games
per arm, with both colors represented. The live gate requires at least three
percentage points of clean uplift, positive lower 95% paired-window interval,
no opponent regression worse than five points and zero own operational failures.
Then the fixed twelve-game private panel and one three-window formal block
must pass, with at least two ranks of one or two and zero own failures. The
fresh 800-root local confirmation remains a reported secondary benchmark.

Existing exact-source and semantic operational bans remain permanent. An own
failure stops that source and requires incumbent restoration and complete
archive/classification. Strength losses guide further work within 8/8.
Weekly usage must remain below 70%; unknown usage defers a wake. The existing
queue, ten-worker/18-GiB limit and one numerical thread are retained. Authoritative
tests run in isolation. New exclusions extend the validated ancestry layers.
Private game transcripts, checkpoints and host-specific control state stay local.
