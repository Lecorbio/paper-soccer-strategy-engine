# Historical results

Most run output is local and ignored by Git. The tracked collections here retain
selected evidence for two completed experiments; they are not required to play
the game or build the core engine.

| Collection | Contents | Starting point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `jacek_arena_bfm/` | Fresh-arena data derivations, model selections, and paired comparisons | [Bot overview](../submissions/codingame/bots/jacek_arena_bfm/README.md) |
| `rank_4_jacek_hybrid/` | Hybrid search, frontier ranking, ablations, and evaluation records | [Experiment record](rank_4_jacek_hybrid/README.md) |

For shorter result summaries, start with [Benchmarks](../benchmarks/README.md)
or the [experiment catalog](../docs/campaigns.md). The [history guide](../docs/history.md)
explains how source hashes, paired games, and archived records support the
reported conclusions.

Keep these files at their recorded paths. Models, manifests, and historical
reports can refer to both their contents and their locations. Private games,
training checkpoints, transcripts, and temporary run directories remain outside
the published collections.
